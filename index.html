Fletchline

Hit three perfect 10s to clear a level. Level 1 is a warm-up past moving poles at 70 m. After that your bow and the target both move for every arrow, and each level stretches the range, from 80 m up to 140 m, with more wind. The game ends when all five levels are cleared. Finish in as few arrows as you can.

DRAW

Press anywhere and drag back. Farther back means more power.

AIM

Drag up or down to tilt the bow. The dotted guide ignores wind, so allow for it.

LOOSE

Release to shoot. Tiny pulls are cancelled.
